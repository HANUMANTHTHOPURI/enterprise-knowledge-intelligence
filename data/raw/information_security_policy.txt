INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines security requirements for employees accessing company systems, applications, and confidential information.

2. AUTHENTICATION

Employees must use company-approved authentication mechanisms when accessing corporate systems.

Multi-factor authentication is required for remote access, administrative accounts, and systems containing confidential information.

Employees must not share passwords or authentication credentials with other individuals.

3. COMPANY DEVICES

Confidential company information must be accessed using company-approved devices unless an exception has been authorized by Information Security.

Devices must have supported operating systems, current security updates, endpoint protection, and disk encryption enabled.

4. REMOTE ACCESS

Remote access to internal company systems must use company-approved secure connections.

Employees must not access confidential systems using unsecured public Wi-Fi unless connected through the approved corporate VPN.

5. SECURITY INCIDENTS

Suspected phishing, credential theft, malware infections, lost company devices, or unauthorized access must be reported to the Information Security team as soon as possible.

Employees should not attempt to independently investigate or remediate a suspected security incident unless instructed by Information Security.

6. DATA HANDLING

Confidential information must only be stored in approved company systems.

Employees must not transfer confidential company data to personal email accounts, personal cloud-storage services, or unauthorized external devices.

7. POLICY REVIEW

Information Security reviews this policy annually and following significant changes to the company's technology or threat environment.
