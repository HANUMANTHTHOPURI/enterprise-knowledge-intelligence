DATA RETENTION AND RECORDS POLICY

1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes requirements for retaining, protecting, and disposing of company business records.

2. RECORD CLASSIFICATION

Business records must be classified according to their legal, regulatory, operational, and contractual requirements.

Employees must store official business records in approved company repositories.

3. RETENTION PERIODS

Business records must be retained according to the company's approved retention schedule.

Employees must not delete records before the applicable retention period expires.

4. LEGAL HOLDS

When Legal issues a legal hold, affected records must be preserved even if their normal retention period has expired.

Employees receiving a legal hold notice must not delete, alter, overwrite, or destroy affected information until Legal releases the hold.

5. DUPLICATE AND TRANSITORY INFORMATION

Duplicate copies and transitory information that are not official business records may be deleted when they no longer have business value, provided they are not subject to a legal hold.

6. SECURE DISPOSAL

Records that have reached the end of their required retention period must be disposed of using approved secure-destruction procedures.

Confidential records must not be placed in ordinary trash or transferred to unauthorized storage systems.

7. RESPONSIBILITIES

Legal and Compliance maintains retention requirements.

Business departments are responsible for ensuring that records under their control are retained and disposed of according to this policy.
