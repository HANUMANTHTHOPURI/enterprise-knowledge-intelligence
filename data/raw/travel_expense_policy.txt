BUSINESS TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY

1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes requirements for business travel expenses and employee reimbursement.

2. TRAVEL APPROVAL

Business travel must be approved by the employee's manager before reservations are made.

International business travel also requires approval from the employee's department director.

3. AIR TRAVEL

Employees should book economy-class airfare for domestic and international business travel.

Premium cabin travel requires documented business justification and approval from the department director.

4. HOTEL EXPENSES

Employees should select reasonably priced accommodations near the business location.

Hotel charges that exceed established regional limits require manager approval and written justification.

5. MEALS

Reasonable meal expenses incurred during approved business travel are reimbursable.

Alcohol and personal entertainment expenses are not reimbursable.

6. EXPENSE REPORTS

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 calendar days after completion of the business trip.

Receipts are required for individual expenses of 25 dollars or more.

7. PERSONAL EXPENSES

Personal purchases, travel upgrades without approval, minibar charges, and expenses for accompanying family members are not reimbursable.
