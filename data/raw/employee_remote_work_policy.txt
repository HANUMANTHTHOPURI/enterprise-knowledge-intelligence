REMOTE WORK POLICY

1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes guidelines for employees who perform their duties from locations outside company offices.

2. ELIGIBILITY

Full-time employees who have completed at least 90 days of employment may request a hybrid work arrangement.

Employees must maintain satisfactory performance and comply with all information-security requirements.

3. HYBRID WORK SCHEDULE

Eligible employees may work remotely up to three days per week.

Employees must work from their designated company office at least two days per week unless an exception has been approved by Human Resources.

Individual departments may establish additional in-office requirements based on operational needs.

4. APPROVAL PROCESS

Employees requesting a hybrid work arrangement must submit a request to their direct manager.

The manager evaluates the request based on job responsibilities, performance, team requirements, and operational needs.

Final approval must be recorded by Human Resources.

5. INFORMATION SECURITY

Employees working remotely must use company-approved devices and secure network connections.

Access to confidential company information must comply with company security and data-protection policies.

6. INTERNATIONAL REMOTE WORK

Working remotely from another country requires prior approval from Human Resources, Information Security, and the Legal department.

Approval must be obtained before the employee begins working outside the employee's assigned country.

7. POLICY REVIEW

Human Resources reviews this policy annually and may revise the policy based on business, legal, or security requirements.
